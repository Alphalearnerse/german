⚖️ SECTION 8: THE ACCUSATIVE (Akkusativ) — Fully Explained

8.1 What IS the accusative?

The accusative marks the direct object — the thing that receives the action of the verb.

English does this with word order: "The dog bites the man." We know the man is the object because of position.
German does it with endings — word order is flexible because the article tells you the role!

7.1 How It Works

German sentences with a modal verb have two parts forming a "bracket" around the middle:

POSITION 1   │  POSITION 2   │     MITTELFELD (middle field)      │  SATZENDE
Subject/Adverb│ Modal verb    │ everything else (objects, adverbs) │  INFINITIVE
────┬─────────┼───────────────┼────────────────────────────────────┼───────────
 Du │ kannst  │ wirklich sehr gut        Gitarre         │  spielen.
────┴─────────┴────────────────────────────────────┴───────────

#TypeGermanEnglishStructure
